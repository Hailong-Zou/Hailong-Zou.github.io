# Education emblem sources

The emblem images in this directory are used as subtle, decorative identifiers for Hailong Zou's education history.

- `pku-seal.png`: Peking University Visual Identity Office, official circular emblem package: <https://vim.pku.edu.cn/xzzq/>
- `ucas-seal.png`: University of Chinese Academy of Sciences official news site: <https://news.ucas.ac.cn/ywsd/c76c2304be2a406aa28c5c1d26c77d06.htm>
- `imecas-seal.png`: Institute of Microelectronics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, official emblem page: <https://www.ime.ac.cn/qt/sjxxsbxt/sjxxsb-sh/>
- `whu-seal.png`: Wuhan University official identity page: <https://www.whu.edu.cn/xxgk/wdbs.htm>

The downloaded raster artwork was only cropped and resized for web delivery; the emblem geometry and colors were not redrawn.
